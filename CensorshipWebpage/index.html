Internet Censorship Answer

Knowledge and Understanding

Internet Censorship is the act of prohibiting or blocking certain sites or posts from users as these sites or posts portray inappropriate information such as radical ideas, pornography, terrorism etc.

Application

The blocking of thes sites or ideas can be for positive reasons such as for children to have restricted access so they cannot see inappropriate sites.
Also goverments use censorship to block radical political ideologies and terrorism so that people don't think these views are true.
But also countries governments can use censorship for bad reasons too, such as in China there is censoring of the Tiananmen Square massacre.
This was a student led protest for democracy, which resulted in troops killing the protestors. This has been censored in China as it displays the negative view of the chinese goverment.

Evaluation

In conclusion Interent Censorship can be positive and negative, but I think censorship is good morally, but countries can use censorship in very immoral reasons. So internet censorship need to be regulated properly.
